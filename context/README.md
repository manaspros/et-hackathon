# Context — My ET Project Documentation

Everything a team member, judge, or contributor needs to understand this project.

## The One-Line Pitch

> ET gives you 20 articles and says "read." My ET gives you 3 briefing items with YOUR portfolio numbers and says "ask me anything."

## The 5 Layers

1. **Account Aggregator** — Your actual financial life powers everything
2. **News Navigator** — CORE: Personalized briefings + Ask ET chat (replaces the article feed)
3. **Financial Trajectory** — Anticipatory content for where you're GOING
4. **Story Arc Tracker** — Visual narratives for any business story
5. **PeakMoment AI** — Smart paywall that fires at peak engagement
