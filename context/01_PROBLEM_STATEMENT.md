## Our Answer: "My ET — Financial Life Newsroom"

A toggle demo: **ET Today** (how ET works now) vs **My ET** (what ET becomes with AI).

Same layout. Same colors. Same branding. Different intelligence underneath.

The difference:
- **ET Today**: Same homepage for 30M users. Feed of static articles. Paywall after 3 articles. No portfolio awareness. You READ passively.
- **My ET**: Opens with a personalized intelligence briefing ("3 things affecting your portfolio today"). You can drill down, ask questions, explore story arcs. Content prepares you for where you're going financially. Paywall fires at peak engagement — not after a count.

## The Core Insight

> Articles are 2005. Briefings are 2026.
